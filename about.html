About StoryCast

Every Story Has the Power to Connect Us.

StoryCast brings human experiences to life through accessible films, audio, and written stories.

Our mission

Listening is a radical act.

We work with communities to record their stories in their own words — then we make those stories available to everyone, regardless of how they see, hear, or navigate the web.

Our accessibility commitment

  • Captions & subtitles

    Every film ships with accurate, human-checked captions.

  • Transcripts

    Full text for every video and audio story, with speaker labels.

  • Keyboard navigation

    Every control works with Tab, Enter and Space.

  • Screen-reader ready

    Semantic structure and meaningful labels throughout.

  • High contrast

    Text meets WCAG 2.1 AA contrast; type is set for reading.

  • Any screen

    Layouts adapt from phones to large desktops.
