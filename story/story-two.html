Mother Hood

A mother, a rocket launch, and the people who wait for her at home.

“Mother Hood” is a short film trailer about what it means to be a mother and be far from home. The footage moves between a snowy road, children in a swimming pool, a mother in a spacesuit before a launch, and a child’s painting of the rocket.

The voices in the trailer talk about letters, guilt and love: “When you miss something, you really begin to realize how much you love it.” The trailer ends on a card that says the film is coming soon.
