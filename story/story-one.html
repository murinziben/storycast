A Journey of Courage

When a fire took everything Amara had built over twenty years, her neighbours didn’t let her story end there.

Director
N. Mbeki
Location
Johannesburg
Runtime
12 minutes

Amara opened her ceramics workshop in 2004. It was a small room with one kiln and a window that let in the morning light. Over two decades it became a gathering place.

In 2024 an electrical fault started a fire overnight. By morning only the walls remained. This film follows the year after — the slow work of starting again, and the community that rebuilt alongside her.

It’s a story about craft, but more than that, it’s about what we owe each other.
